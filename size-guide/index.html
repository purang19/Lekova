How to Measure

  • Chest: around the fullest part, under the arms.
  • Waist: around your natural waistline.
  • Hip: around the fullest part of your hips.
  • Inseam: from the crotch seam to the bottom of the leg.

Measure a garment you already own and love, then compare it to the tables above for the closest match.
