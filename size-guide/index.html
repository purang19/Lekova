How to Measure

  • Chest: across the chest at the fullest point.
  • Length: from the highest point of the shoulder straight down to the hem.
  • Shoulder width: from shoulder seam to shoulder seam.
  • Arm length: from the shoulder seam to the end of the sleeve.

Easiest check: measure a t-shirt you already own and love, then compare it to the table above for the closest match.
