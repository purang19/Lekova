Our Story

Wearable Art

Le Kova makes original graphic tees — each one a small story printed on soft, quality cotton.

Every design starts as an illustration, not a logo. Some come from the legends and landscapes of the Andaman Sea; others from a bit of everyday attitude. The goal is always the same: a tee you reach for because it says something.

Rooted in Khao Lak

We're inspired by Khao Lak, Thailand — its coastline, its wildlife, and its easy sense of escape. That spirit runs through our designs, from a mermaid of the Andaman to an elephant on holiday. Wearable souvenirs of a place, and a feeling.

Designed In-House

Every graphic is drawn by us and printed with care on comfortable, durable tees made to be worn again and again. We release designs in small drops, so there's always something new to discover.
